Android horror · Single-player · By 1900xd

Take the key.
The lights go out.

Learn the way through an empty building. Then survive the journey back. NOCLIP is a Backrooms escape game with 100 themed levels, generated layouts and a chase that starts when you take the key.

Play free on Google Play ↗ Watch actual gameplay ↓

Offline play. No ads or game account. Every level can unlock through play; an optional one-time purchase unlocks them all immediately.

The familiar route becomes the dangerous one

Explore first. Escape second.

01 / EXPLORE

Find your bearings.

Scout rooms, corridors and floors while the lights are on. Find the key and learn the route back to the exit.

02 / TAKE THE KEY

Everything changes.

The exit unlocks. The power fails. A monster starts chasing you through the building you just explored.

03 / GET BACK

Remember the way.

Use tags, fading footprints and a floor-by-floor radar/map to retrace your steps and reach the door.

100 themed levels

A fixed progression through offices, hotels, Poolrooms and stranger places, with generated layouts within each theme.

Your pace, your controls

Adjust monster speed, stamina regeneration and the camcorder effect. Touch controls include a left-handed layout.
