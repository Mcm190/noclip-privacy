# NOCLIP

**Developer:** 1900xd (solo developer)  
**Released platform:** Android / Google Play  
**Engine:** Godot  
**Genre:** single-player, first-person Backrooms escape horror  
**Price:** free to play; optional one-time purchase to unlock every level immediately  
**Store:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.noclip.backrooms&hl=en_GB  
**Public developer contact:** maxcartermarshall@gmail.com

Explore a building, find the floating key and learn your route to the exit. Taking the key opens the door, cuts the power and wakes a pursuing monster. Tags, fading footprints and a floor-by-floor radar/map help you retrace your steps.

NOCLIP has 100 themed levels in a fixed progression, with generated layouts and a final ending. The settings range from offices, hotels and Poolrooms to stranger places. Buildings include multiple floors, stairs, ramps and galleries. Environmental sounds, failing lights and a camcorder effect build tension before the chase.

It is playable offline, without ads or a game account. The first three levels are open immediately; the remaining levels unlock through play. The optional one-time purchase skips that unlocking process. Monster speed and stamina regeneration are adjustable, and touch controls support a left-handed layout.
